## Slide 2: The Problem

### Construction is Broken

**The €2.8 Trillion Problem:**

1. **Cost Crisis** — Traditional construction costs have risen 47% since 2020. Average home now €4,800/m² in Poland.

2. **Speed Crisis** — 6-12 months to build a single-family home. Developers hemorrhage capital waiting.

3. **Labor Crisis** — 25% of skilled construction workers retiring by 2030. No replacement pipeline.

4. **Climate Crisis** — Construction accounts for 39% of global CO₂ emissions. EU mandates near-zero buildings by 2030.

*"The industry that houses humanity is simultaneously failing on cost, speed, labor, and sustainability."*

## Slide 3: The Solution

### The Comb-Beam Revolution

**Patented interlocking wood-panel system that eliminates traditional construction's core inefficiencies.**

- **No structural timber** — Uses commodity MFP/OSB boards
- **No concrete foundations** — Lightweight system works on simple footings
- **No specialized labor** — Two people with basic power tools
- **No thermal bridges** — Uniform insulation, passive-house standard as default

**One technology. Four breakthroughs.**

## Slide 4: How It Works

### Precision Manufacturing Meets Simple Assembly

**Phase 1: Design** (1-2 weeks)
Customized architectural design using our component library. BIM-ready, permit-optimized.

**Phase 2: Manufacture** (2-3 weeks)
CNC-cut panels with interlocking comb-beam joints. Factory-integrated insulation. Zero waste.

**Phase 3: Deliver**
Flat-packed components transported to site. Single truck delivers entire home.

**Phase 4: Build** (1-4 weeks)
Snap-together assembly. No cranes. No concrete trucks. Two people, basic tools.

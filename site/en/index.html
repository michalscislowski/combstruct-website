Before you choose
Combstruct.

Materials, safety, durability and financing. Answers to questions before you build.

Talk to us about your home
What about fire safety in an OSB or plywood house?+

We design protection around the whole structure, specifying fire-protective linings to shield the OSB or plywood core. Appropriate joints and service penetration details complete the protection. According to our own test results, the tested wall, intermediate-floor and ground-floor assemblies achieve REI 60: they maintain load-bearing capacity, integrity and insulation for 60 minutes under the test conditions.

What about rain and moisture?+

Protective layers shield the structure. We specify moisture-resistant boards and, in areas exposed to water, appropriate seals and waterproofing. Protection also includes board joints, flashings and service penetrations.

How long will the house last?+

We select high-quality materials and structural solutions with a view to more than 50 years of use. Correct assembly, appropriate maintenance and regular inspections are essential to preserving the home’s durability for decades to come.

What warranty applies?+

Combstruct provides a 30-year warranty. The warranty document for the selected option defines coverage, use and maintenance conditions, and how to report defects. Check which components and work are covered before entering into the contract. The warranty does not limit statutory customer rights.

Can a mortgage finance the build?+

Confirm financing with your chosen lender before ordering. The lender assesses the borrower, property, project documentation and loan security. Present the design, cost estimate and payment schedule, particularly if the material kit is paid for before assembly. We do not claim automatic acceptance of Combstruct by every bank.

Does the system have a patent and certificates?+

The comb beam, structural element and load-bearing core are described in international patent application WO 2025/264131 A1. Publication of an application is not a technical certificate for a building. Material performance declarations, structural calculations and assembly classifications apply to specific products and designs and must be checked for the selected project.

Can I move in after 1–2 weeks?+

This estimate covers structural assembly for a 120 m² home. The full schedule also includes design, approvals, site preparation and the remaining work needed before moving in.

Can I customise the house?+

Yes. The structural components repeat, while the layout, building form and facade are tailored to each design. Bedrooms, a home office and terrace access are planned around the structural grid and the conditions of your plot.

Where do I start?+

Explore the available homes, compare floor plans and choose a materials-only kit or a kit with assembly. Once you find a home you like, talk to us about adapting it to your plot and needs.

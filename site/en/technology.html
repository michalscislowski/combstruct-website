A building system
ready for the AI era.

Repeatable components. Clear connection rules. Data ready for automation. Combstruct Flow brings building technology and software tools together for designers, developers, sheet manufacturers and construction partners.

Example: Combstruct 30

    Floor plans in.
    Production order out.

    We are developing the Combstruct AI model to turn building designs into comb-board structures. Automatic adaptation to the system grid, component schedules and precise production orders — in one process.

    Less manual redrawing and data entry. A shorter route from design to cut components and assembly.

    One system.
    Partners at every stage.

    Developers and investors

    Adapt your own designs, plan materials and use the same standard across multiple sites. Make orders easier to plan and repeat projects easier to deliver.

    Sheet and component manufacturers

    OSB, MFP and plywood become identified parts of a specific building. Model data defines what to cut, how many parts to make and how to prepare them for delivery.

    Designers and contractors

    A shared model with component IDs and clear connections. From adapting the design to preparing site packages and the assembly sequence.
